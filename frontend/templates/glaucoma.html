About Glaucoma

Learn about glaucoma, its causes, symptoms, and the importance of early detection.

What is Glaucoma?

Glaucoma is an eye disease that damages the optic nerve. It usually develops due to increased eye pressure and may lead to permanent vision loss if not detected early.

Symptoms

  • Blurred vision
  • Loss of peripheral vision
  • Eye pain
  • Headache
  • Halos around lights
  • Nausea (in severe cases)

Risk Factors

  • Age above 40 years
  • Family history of glaucoma
  • High eye pressure
  • Diabetes
  • High blood pressure
  • Long-term steroid use

Prevention

  • Regular eye examinations
  • Maintain healthy blood pressure
  • Control diabetes
  • Follow doctor's advice
  • Protect your eyes from injury
